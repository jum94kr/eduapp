# 12-Week Learning Progression (Grade 2, Phonics RPG)

## Weekly Structure
- 5 sessions/week, 15–20 min each.
- Session recipe: Adventure (5m) + Discovery (5m) + Story/Battle (5–10m).
- Built-in review every 3rd session.

## Roadmap
1. **Week 1**: CVC blending refresh (`a,e,i,o,u` short vowels), simple onset-rime.
2. **Week 2**: Initial blends (`sn, sm, st, sp`) in playful contexts.
3. **Week 3**: L-blends (`bl, cl, fl, gl, pl, sl`) + story reinforcement.
4. **Week 4**: R-blends (`br, cr, dr, fr, gr, tr`) with speed stabilization.
5. **Week 5**: Digraphs I (`sh, ch, th, wh`) + articulation guidance.
6. **Week 6**: Digraphs II (`ck, ng, ph`) + mixed review boss zone.
7. **Week 7**: Silent-e long vowels (`a_e, i_e, o_e, u_e`).
8. **Week 8**: Vowel teams I (`ai, ee, oa, ie`) in context-rich scenes.
9. **Week 9**: Vowel teams II (`ea, ay, oi, oy`) with story mission chaining.
10. **Week 10**: R-controlled vowels (`ar, or, er, ir, ur`).
11. **Week 11**: Complex clusters in connected text + battle skill unlocks.
12. **Week 12**: Mastery arc: mixed-world quests, confidence celebration, cosmetic season rewards.

## Promotion Criteria
- Promotion from week zone requires:
  - Pattern mastery >= 70 for current cluster family.
  - At least 3 successful context reads over 2 days.
  - Frustration guard not triggered (adaptive fallback if triggered).
